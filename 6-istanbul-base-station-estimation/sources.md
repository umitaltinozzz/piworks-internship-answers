## Source Notes

**BTK TBMM report (Jan 2020):** The original document resides in the TBMM Petition Commission archive. The figures used here were accessed via press coverage. This source is ranked highest because it provides province-level breakdowns for three cities simultaneously, enabling direct share calculations.

**Dec 2015 Istanbul figure:** The original parliamentary record was not directly accessed; the value was sourced from press coverage of Minister Yıldırım's official response. Treated as high-reliability given its origin as a formal parliamentary statement.

**2019 Istanbul figure:** Two different values (46,482 and 46,842) appear in separate press reports citing the same ministerial response. Without access to the original text, the correct figure cannot be confirmed. Excluded from all calculations.

**International comparisons (London, Seoul):** Open-source estimates derived from operator reports and regulatory filings. Used only as a final sanity check; not incorporated into the core estimate.

---

## Limitations

The primary gap in this analysis is the absence of publicly available province-level BTK data for 2021–2025. Official annual reports covering this period would narrow the confidence interval considerably. Additional data that would improve the estimate includes the precise regulatory definition of "base station" (physical site vs. operator-antenna unit), operator annual reports for cross-validation, and operator investment commitments from the 5G auction to sharpen the 5G delta projection.
